Frequently asked questions

What Lethe forgets, and what it can't.

Has a real swap run?

No: BLOCKED. A live quote creates a mainnet deposit address and needs mainnet ZEC, which waits on the maintainer's sign-off. The quotes are real dry quotes; the testnet refunds are stand-ins.

Is the key derivation final?

No. zakura-one-time-ivk 0.0.1-rc1 is a draft convention that must not be used to issue live addresses before review. Mainnet addresses here appear only in dry quotes.

What can still link my swaps?

Amounts, timing and the recipient on the destination chain. Lethe removes the refund-address link only.

Can I spend the refunds?

They stay spendable by the wallet, but spending one-time notes needs wallet integration, not done here. Not audited. A portfolio piece; the Zecathon entry is Kage (Shielded x402).

Let the river take it

One-time keys in Rust, a compact-block scanner, a CLI and an MCP server. The tests call the real 1Click API with dry quotes and scan real testnet blocks.
