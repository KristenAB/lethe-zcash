zswap

Three swaps. Nothing links them.

A swap out of Zcash through NEAR Intents names a refund address, and the provider marks the request public. zswap gives every swap a one-time refund address with its own viewing key, so those public fields cannot tie your swaps together.

Limits

No real swap yet (mainnet ZEC needed). The one-time key derivation is a draft convention awaiting review. Amounts and timing can still link swaps.
